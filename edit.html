About the Program

The Excellence in Detector and Instrumentation Technologies (EDIT) school is a prestigious training program aimed at young researchers, aimed to deepen their knowledge of detector technologies and instrumentation for particle physics.

Through a combination of academic courses and hands-on laboratories, the curriculum covers the complete signal chain—from the interaction of particles with matter to the electronic readout and final signal processing.

Electrotechnical & Instrumentation Focus

My primary motivation for attending EDIT 2026 is to bridge the gap between physical sensor hardware and high-level data analysis. Understanding the electrotechnical properties of detectors is crucial for designing robust robotic and measurement systems.

  • Front-End Electronics: Studying the design of low-noise pre-amplifiers, shapers, and digitization circuits (ADCs). The course dives into how minute analogue charges generated in sensors are converted into robust digital signals suitable for data acquisition.
  • Signal Processing: Techniques for filtering noise, baseline restoration, and feature extraction directly at the hardware level. This includes understanding the impact of capacitance and resistance in silicon and gaseous detectors on signal timing and resolution.
  • Detector Technologies: Detailed analysis of Silicon Detectors, Gaseous Detectors, and Noble Liquid Detectors, focusing on their electrical characteristics (breakdown voltage, drift velocity, charge collection efficiency).
  • Data Acquisition (DAQ): Integration of front-end electronics with high-speed data transmission systems, triggering logic, and real-time processing architectures often implemented on FPGAs.
