Program Overview

EDIT 2026 is a technical school focusing on the hardware and electronics of particle physics experiments. The curriculum covers the full signal processing chain: from sensor ionization and charge collection to analog signal conditioning, digitization, and data acquisition.

Engineering & Instrumentation Modules

  • Silicon Detectors

    Analysis of semiconductor sensor properties: depletion voltage, leakage current, and capacitance. Comparison of Hybrid Pixel Sensors vs. Monolithic Active Pixel Sensors (MAPS/CMOS). Focus on bump-bonding interconnects and radiation-hard design layout.

  • Front-End Electronics & Signal Processing

    Design of low-noise analog readout chains. Topics include Charge Sensitive Amplifiers (CSA), pulse shaping (CR-RC filters), and noise matching (ENC optimization). Implementation of discriminators, ADCs, and TDCs (Time-to-Digital Converters) for high-precision timing.

  • Gaseous Detectors

    Engineering of Micro-Pattern Gaseous Detectors (MPGDs) and GEM (Gas Electron Multiplier) foils. Focus on high-voltage biasing, electric field shaping, avalanche amplification mechanics, and signal induction on readout strips.

  • Photon & Noble Liquid Detectors

    Characterization of photo-detectors: PMTs vs. SiPMs (Silicon Photomultipliers). Analysis of dark count rate, cross-talk, and quantum efficiency. Operations of Time Projection Chambers (TPC) in cryogenic liquid argon/xenon environments and related cold electronics.

  • Modelling & Simulation

    Finite Element Method (FEM) and TCAD simulations for electric field distribution and charge transport within sensors. SPICE simulations for transient analysis of the analog front-end and readout circuitry.
